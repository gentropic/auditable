\chapter{Keys and mouse}\label{app:keys}

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lL@{}}
\toprule
\sffamily\bfseries input & \sffamily\bfseries does \\
\midrule
\key{Ctrl}+\key{O} & open a file \\
\key{Ctrl}+\key{F} & find in the table (\cref{sec:find}) \\
\key{Enter}, \key{Shift}+\key{Enter} in find & next, previous match \\
\key{Esc} in find & close it \\
\key{Enter} in the filter box & apply the filter \\
\key{Esc} in the filter box & clear the filter \\
\key{↑} \key{↓} \key{Tab} in a suggestion list & move through it, take one \\
\key{Ctrl}+\key{Z} in the filter box & the filter it held before \\
\key{Ctrl}+\key{C} & copy the selection as tab-separated text \\
\key{Esc} during a scan & cancel it \\
\key{Enter} in the row box & jump to that row \\
\key{↑} \key{↓} in the record inspector & the previous, next row \\
\key{Enter}, \key{Esc} in the calculated-column editor & add the column, cancel \\
\midrule
click a header & sort: ascending, descending, off \\
right-click a header & sort, then-by, statistics, filter by, text or number, number format, colour scale,
hide and show, calculated columns, autofit \\
right-click a cell & copy (with header, with row \#), filter by this value, statistics, inspect the row \\
drag a header's right border & resize the column; double-click it to fit \\
click, drag across a selection & select cells; \key{Shift}-click extends \\
hover a distribution & read the value under the pointer \\
click a distribution & filter to that bar or value \\
drag across a distribution & filter to the range or values \\
double-click a distribution & the column's statistics \\
click the kind badge & how the file is read (\cref{sec:interpretation}) \\
click \ui{⚠ encoding?} & the same, for the encoding \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Scrolling is locked to one axis per gesture unless \menu{View>Lock scroll to one axis} is off. On a Mac,
\key{⌘} stands for \key{Ctrl}.
